This site collects plain-English summaries of peer-reviewed research on exercise: lifting, cardio, supplements and recovery. It is a sister site to [Contemplative Neuroscience](https://contemplativeneuroscience.com), which does the same for research on meditation and the brain.

Each summary explains one published paper: why the researchers asked their question, what they did, what they found, and how much weight the result can bear. The goal is to make the research accessible without dumbing it down.

If you're curious about who I am beyond this project, you can learn more on my [personal site](https://www.craigmbooth.com).
